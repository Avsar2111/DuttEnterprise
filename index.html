Corporate Framework

Your Trusted Technical Sourcing Backbone

Established in 1990, DUTT ENTERPRISE has rapidly emerged as a trusted name in the field of laboratory glassware, serving customers with a commitment to quality, reliability, and customer satisfaction. Based in Anand, Gujarat, India, we are engaged as a Trader, Wholesaler, and Distributor of an extensive range of laboratory glassware designed to meet the evolving needs of educational institutions, research laboratories, pharmaceutical companies, chemical industries, Dairy Industries, healthcare organizations, and quality control facilities. With over 36 years of industry experience, we have built our reputation by supplying laboratory products that combine precision, durability, and dependable performance. Our comprehensive product portfolio includes Beakers, Bottles, Burettes, Condensers, Cylinders, Flasks, Desiccators, Extraction Apparatus, Distillation Equipment, and numerous other scientific laboratory glassware manufactured from premium heat-resistant SUN Brand Borosilicate Glass. Every product is selected to deliver outstanding resistance to heat, chemicals, and thermal shock, making it suitable for demanding laboratory environments. At DUTT ENTERPRISE, we understand that precision is the foundation of every scientific achievement. Whether supporting educational experiments, pharmaceutical research, industrial testing, or laboratory analysis, we strive to provide products that ensure consistent performance, dimensional accuracy, and long-lasting reliability. Our extensive product range enables us to offer complete laboratory glassware solutions under one roof, helping customers source quality products with confidence and convenience. Our business philosophy is built on Quality, Integrity, Reliability, and Customer Satisfaction. We believe in establishing long-term relationships by delivering superior products, competitive pricing, prompt service, and professional support. Every order, regardless of size, receives the same level of attention and commitment that has become the hallmark of our organization. Driven by continuous improvement and a customer-centric approach, DUTT ENTERPRISE is committed to becoming a preferred partner for laboratories and scientific institutions across India. As we continue to expand our product portfolio and strengthen our market presence, we remain focused on delivering quality products and dependable laboratory solutions.
